# Code of conduct

Aipim is a small, open design system, made to be used by many people. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, the Figma file or anywhere the project is represented) is expected to make it a respectful place to learn and build.

This text is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith, and say what you mean without attacking the person.
- Welcome beginners and people who write in a second language. Portuguese and English are both fine.
- Give and take feedback on the work, with reasons. A review is about the pull request, not the author.
- Respect other people's time and accessibility needs. If someone asks for a different way to take part, try to give it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind (for example about origin, gender, sexuality, disability, age, religion or experience).
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, use the contact form at <https://talisgalhardi.com/#contato> and say it is about Aipim. Reports are handled privately and in good faith, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.
