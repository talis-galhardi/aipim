# Changelog

All notable changes to Aipim are written here, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## How versions work

Aipim is the tokens (`tokens/`), the web components (`components/web/`), the icons (`icons/`) and the Figma file. They share one version number.

- **Patch** (1.0.1): fixes that change no name, token value or markup you depend on. Safe to take.
- **Minor** (1.1.0): new components, tokens, variants or icons. Nothing existing breaks.
- **Major** (2.0.0): anything that can break your screens. A renamed or removed class, token or icon, changed markup, or a color change large enough to alter contrast pairs.
- **Deprecation.** Before something is removed, it is marked deprecated in this file and in its spec for at least one minor version. The old name keeps working. The entry says what to use instead.
- **Migration notes.** Every major version has a "Migration" section here with before and after for each breaking change.
- Token **values** may change in a minor version when the change is a fix (for example, a color that failed a contrast check). The change is listed under "Changed".

### Added

- Brazilian field (`docs/en/components/brazilian-field.md`, in beta): a text field with `data-aipim-format` for CPF, CNPJ, CEP, phone, dates as dd/mm/aaaa and amounts in reais. `components/web/aipim.js` writes the mask while you type (the caret stays after the character typed, and Backspace on a dot or a dash deletes the digit before it) and checks the value when you leave the field: both check digits of the CPF and of the CNPJ, the CNPJ with letters given out since July 2026, an area code that exists, a mobile number with 9, a date that exists. The error goes in the helper text in Portuguese or English, after the nearest `lang`, and goes away as soon as the value is valid. `Aipim.br.format`, `check` and `clean` do the same as plain functions, for frameworks and servers. It uses the CSS of the text field. In Figma it is a component set made of Text field instances, with its page on Moléculas.
- Select (`docs/en/components/select.md`, in beta): the native `select` with the look of the text field and a chevron, in `components/web/select.css`. The first option with an empty value is a prompt in a muted color.
- Combobox (`docs/en/components/combobox.md`, in beta): a text field that filters a list of options while you type, after the ARIA 1.2 combobox pattern with list autocomplete and manual selection, in `components/web/combobox.css`. `aipim.js` filters without caring for accents ("sao" finds São Paulo, "sp" finds the option whose value is SP), moves the option under the arrows with `aria-activedescendant` while focus stays in the field, chooses with Enter or a click, closes and then clears with Escape, and shows an error when the person leaves with a text that is not an option. The count of options found is read by screen readers after a pause in the typing. The popup opens under the field without a script measuring it: it sits in the grid row of the control. In Figma, Select and Combobox are component sets made from the Text field variants, with their pages on Moléculas; Combobox adds an Open state and a Combobox option set.
- Date picker (`docs/en/components/datepicker.md`, in beta): the date of the Brazilian field with a calendar button inside it, in `components/web/datepicker.css`. Typing stays the main way in; the button opens a calendar that `aipim.js` writes, after the ARIA date picker dialog: the arrows move by day and week, Home and End to the ends of the week, Page Up and Page Down by month (with Shift, by year), Enter or Space chooses, Escape closes, Tab stays inside, and focus goes back to the button. The week starts on Sunday and the names come from `Intl` in the language of the page. Each day is a `button` inside its cell, and no day is in the tab order: the script moves the focus. The ARIA pattern suggests a roving `tabindex`, but the test with VoiceOver in Safari found that an attribute changing on the element being focused loses the announcement, and the day was read out on only some of the arrow presses. `data-aipim-min` and `data-aipim-max` disable the days outside and show an error for a typed date outside them. The button says the date chosen, and a valid date shows its day of the week under the helper text. In Figma it is a component set made from the Combobox variants with a calendar Icon button, plus a Date picker day set and a Date picker calendar, with its page on Moléculas.
- Avatar (`docs/en/components/avatar.md`, in beta): a person or a thing inside a circle, in `components/web/avatar.css`. Three sizes (32, 40 and 48px, from the control sizes), initials on one of the four tones with the text of the tone (`tint/text`), an icon, a photo that fills the circle, and a count ("+3"). `aipim-avatar-group` overlaps the avatars by 6, 8 or 12px with a 3px ring in the color of the page. It only shows: it is not a stop for the keyboard. In Figma it is the sets Avatar and Avatar group, with their page on Átomos.
- List item (`docs/en/components/list-item.md`, in beta): one row of a list, in `components/web/list-item.css`: a start (an avatar), a title with a description, and an end that is a chevron, a value or a switch. A row that leads somewhere is an `a` or a `button` and is one stop for the keyboard, with a focus ring drawn inside; a row with a switch is the `label` of the switch; a row that only shows has no hover and no focus. The divider goes away on the last row. In Figma it is the set List item, with its page on Moléculas.
- Accordion (`docs/en/components/accordion.md`, in beta): the native `details` and `summary` with the look of the system, in `components/web/accordion.css`, with no script. The whole header opens and closes the item, the chevron turns up when it is open, and `details` with the same `name` open one at a time. It has no disabled state, because `details` has none. In Figma it is the set Accordion item, with its page on Moléculas.
- `tools/verify_web.py` checks the avatar and its group (sizes, overlap, ring), the list item (heights, hover only where the row acts, the focus ring, a disabled link, a row with a switch) and the accordion (click, Enter and Space, one open at a time, the chevron) in the three engines.
- `tools/verify_web.py` checks the date picker in the three engines: the keys, Tab kept inside, choosing, the range error, and axe on the open calendar.
- `tools/verify_web.py` checks the select and the combobox in the three engines, and the tabs check allows the 1px WebKit rounds a scroll position to.
- `tools/verify_web.py` checks the Brazilian field in the three engines: typing, pasting a value that came with the page, Backspace on the mask, the check when leaving and the error going away.
- Storybook: the pages **What's new** (a short summary of each version that shipped) and **Roadmap** (what is planned, from the version table of `ROADMAP.md`), in both languages, after the Introduction. `tools/check_releases.py`, in `tools/check.py`, fails when a released version or a planned one is missing from them.
- Two contrast checks, 88 in all: inverse text on the body text color (7:1), and the visited link on a card (4.5:1). The Aipim colors already passed both, so no token changes. The focus ring is not checked against the button: it sits 3px away, on the background, and the checks against the background already cover it.
- Theme builder: a **Random theme** button. It draws a primary, a secondary that matches it by a classic color harmony, status colors in their family and grays that lean to the primary. Every theme it draws passes all the checks; the tones it had to pick come as manual (`randomTheme` in `theme-builder/theme.js`).

- `tools/check.py` (also `npm run check`): every quick check in one command, and the only place that lists them. CI, AGENTS.md and CONTRIBUTING.md call it. `npm run check:browser` runs the ones that need a browser.
- `tools/check_facts.py`: counts the components, icons, illustrations, contrast checks and rules of AGENTS.md from the repository, and fails when a text says another number, in English or Portuguese, in digits or in words. It also checks the release date against CHANGELOG.md.
- `tools/build_specs.py`: writes the light and dark colors of the token tables of the 34 specs from the tokens, so a change in the palette reaches every spec. The translation stamp leaves hex colors out, so this needs no new translation.
- `tools/aipim_tools.py`: what the scripts share (reading the specs, writing or checking a generated file, a local server for the browser checks, the report of checks). `stories/_stories.js` does the same for the 17 story files, which now hold only what is their own.
- Theme builder (`theme-builder/`, online at https://talisgalhardi.com/aipim/theme-builder/): a page to make a theme from your own colors without code. One color per role, nine tones each, and every semantic role on the tone that passes the 84 contrast checks and the color-blindness ones, in light and dark. A tone can be picked by hand. When two colors look alike to someone with color blindness, the builder makes one of them lighter or darker (never a hue the person picked); when something still fails, it suggests a small change that keeps each status color in its family and stays as close as possible to the color picked. "See as" shows the preview through a simulation of protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (the model of the checks, Machado et al. 2009), counts the problems per kind of vision and marks each one on the preview, numbered like the list of checks. A theme with problems can be exported after a confirmation that lists them, asked once per theme; the file carries them in a comment. It shows real components in a live preview, exports a CSS file to load after `aipim.css`, and keeps the theme in its link. With the default colors it gives exactly the colors of `tokens/tokens.json`, and `tools/verify_theme_builder.py` checks that.
- A "Theme builder" page in the Storybook (`stories/en/theme-builder.mdx` and its Portuguese translation), right after the Introduction: the basics in plain words (color, tone, use, check), then the steps for designers (to Figma) and for developers (to CSS), with a button that opens the builder, and a References tab with the tools and the research behind the builder. The Color page and the "Customizable" principle of the Introduction link to it.
- `DocAction` in `.storybook/docs-tabs.js`: the call to action of a docs page, an Aipim button that opens a new tab and says so to screen readers. The docs pages now get the icon sprite from the docs container, so an MDX page with no story can show icons.
- Theme builder in three panels across the whole width: the controls, the preview and the summary of the theme with the export. Each problem on the preview has a frame and a numbered spot whose tooltip (pointer or keyboard) says what fails and why, in the words of the checks; a problem in the checks takes the preview to its theme, kind of vision and mark, and a second click makes the mark blink.
- Theme builder laid out like a checkout. The controls sit on the page background, the preview on a recessed stage, and the summary of the theme in a raised card on the right: the six colors, one line per kind of check with its count, what does not pass, the fix that passes (each color from and to), the status and one main export button. Each color is one field (swatch and hex) next to its nine tones. "See as" and, right above the preview, light and dark are segmented controls, each option with its count of problems as a badge. The text under "See as" keeps one height for every kind of vision, so the preview never moves. Narrower than three panels, a bar at the bottom keeps the status in sight and leads to the summary.
- Theme builder: each row of "Your colors" is the picker of its color. It has the hex, the nine tones as one segmented piece (the kept tone wears a ring and its number), and a Hue and an Intensity bar drawn in the color they set, which change the seed of the scale. A typed hex moves the bars; moving a bar lets go of the kept hex. While a bar is dragged, the summary looks for a fix once the bar stops.
- Aipim Theme, a Figma plugin (`figma-plugin/`): it applies a theme from the builder to the variables of an Aipim file (the colors of Primitives, and where each Semantic role points in Light and Dark). It edits existing variables only. It checks every color before it writes, so a theme edited by hand with a wrong hex changes nothing.
- `storybook` icon in the Platforms group, the Storybook logo drawn for Aipim on the 24px grid with the 1.5px stroke (Hugeicons Free has no Storybook logo). Its source is `icons/custom/storybook.svg`, and `tools/build_icons.py` now reads the icons in `icons/custom/` next to the Hugeicons ones. A link to the Storybook uses it instead of `external-link`. There are 64 icons now.
- `docs/figma-sync-log.md`: what the Figma file has to match in the repository, how to check each part, and a log of every pass. `CLAUDE.md` points to it.
- `aipim-ds/br.js` (`components/web/aipim-br.js`): the masks and the checks of the Brazilian field as a module with named exports, so `format`, `check` and `clean` can be imported on a server, in a framework or in a test. They were reachable only as `window.Aipim.br` before, which no server has. The module is the one source: `tools/build_web.py` copies it into `components/web/aipim.js` between the `br:` markers, with `export` dropped, because a file loaded with `<script>` cannot carry the keyword. The browser file and the module can no longer disagree.
- `tools/check_breakpoints.py`, in `tools/check.py`: every width in a media query has to come from the breakpoint scale (`BREAKPOINT` in `tools/build_tokens.py`: 600, 1024, 1440). CSS cannot read a token in a media query, so the widths are written as numbers and nothing kept them together. A width outside the scale needs a `breakpoint: <width>` comment above the rule saying what it answers to instead, and the number in the comment has to be the one in the query.

### Fixed

- The version every generated file carries (`--aipim-version` in the CSS, the Kotlin and Swift constants, and the header of `tokens.json` and the six generated docs) was a copy of `package.json`'s version in `tools/build_tokens.py`, not a read of it: it had stopped moving at `0.1.2`, through three releases including 1.0.0, and nothing noticed. The reason nothing noticed is the deeper fix: `build_tokens.py --check` only ran the contrast and color-blindness math and exited, never comparing its ten output files to what was on disk, despite `tools/check.py` describing that line as checking "the tokens are up to date". It now does, the same way the other generators already did, and reads the version from `package.json` instead of carrying its own.
- `ROADMAP.md` and `docs/figma-sync-log.md` still said the Brazilian icon set ships in v1.1, the plan before the components moved to v1.2 on 2026-10-27. `ROADMAP.md`'s own version table, two lines above the one that was wrong, already said v1.2.
- `docs/en/verification.md` said "84 checks" and "Last verified: 2026-10-07", a count and a date from before the palette grew to 88 checks on 2026-10-26. The Portuguese translation had already been corrected that day; the English source, which the translation is supposed to follow, had not. `tools/check_facts.py` never caught it because its pattern for a bare "checks" count requires a word like "contrast" next to it, which "84 checks" alone did not have; the line now reads "88 contrast checks", which closes that gap for the future. Re-verified for real before writing today's date: `python3 tools/verify_web.py` passes on the current components.
- Three sentences written before the 1.0.0 release stayed after it shipped, each now moot or self-contradictory: `CHANGELOG.md`'s "Until 1.0.0, the minor version may include breaking changes" (two lines after this file's own "Minor: ... Nothing existing breaks"), the same sentence in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `SECURITY.md`'s "Until 1.0.0 that is the newest 0.x" (only 1.0.0 is published now). Removed or reworded.
- `components/web/aipim.js` on a server: the file is in the package's `exports`, and an `import` of it in Next, Astro or SvelteKit stopped the build with `ReferenceError: document is not defined`, because it read `document` while it loaded. It now leaves without doing anything when there is no page, and the components keep working from the CSS alone until the markup reaches a browser.
- Media queries now break on the scale and are written as ranges: `@media (width < 600px)` in place of `max-width:599.98px` in the modal, which needed the decimal to not overlap the rule above it by a pixel. The theme builder and the Storybook docs pages answered to 620, 640, 900, 1101 and 1280. The phone widths became 600; the 900 and the 1280 of the theme builder stay, with the reason written above them, because that page is a three-pane tool and its panes fit at those widths and not where the scale says. The docs pages' 1101 became 1149, the width where 8 of the 12 columns truly meet the 68ch measure: between the two the body text was 694px instead of 726px, narrower than the design asks for.
- Storybook code blocks in dark: a keyword (`import`, `from`, `export`) was Prism's purple `#b474dd` on the code background, 4.36:1 where 4.5:1 is needed. The same color was already corrected for attribute values, but a keyword only reached a docs page once one of them wrote an `import`, since Prism tags it `token keyword module`. It now takes the link role, which passes in both modes.
- Eight icons (`add`, `edit`, `external-link`, `home`, `image`, `mail`, `settings`, `user`) left `theme-builder/index.html`, where they were inlined and used by nothing. The generator kept whatever a page already had, so the inlined set could only grow. It now writes only what the markup points at and what a page declares its scripts pick, and it fails when a script names an icon that is in neither, so a missed one shows up as an error instead of a hole in the layout.
- The helper/error text of every text-like field (`aipim-field__helper`) is now `aria-live="polite"`. Found with VoiceOver in Safari on the combobox: leaving the field with a text that is not an option set the error silently, because by the time `aipim.js` wrote it on `blur` the focus had already moved to the next field; the error only came up if the person tabbed back. Affects the combobox, the Brazilian field and any field whose error is set after the field loses focus.

- The component pages of the Storybook showed 2026-10-06 as the last verification, and `verification.md` says 2026-10-07. The date is now read from `verification.md`.
- The list of checks in CONTRIBUTING.md had left out the Storybook check.

- Figma: the page about themes and the Ilustrações page said the illustrations follow the theme colors. They do not yet: the ten illustrations have fixed colors, and following the theme is planned for v1.1.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-13

The first stable release: 17 components, the tokens, 63 icons, 10 illustrations, the Figma file in the Figma Community, the Storybook in Brazilian Portuguese and English, and the package for AI agents.

### Added

Components and tokens
- 17 web components in HTML and CSS: button, icon button, link, badge, tag, checkbox, radio, switch, text field, alert, toast, tabs, card, empty state, top bar, tab bar and modal. One CSS file each, joined in `aipim-components.css`, and an optional `aipim.js` (dismiss buttons, tabs, the modal fallback, and `Aipim.init(container)` for content added after the page loaded). Each has a spec in `docs/en/components/`: when to use, anatomy, variants, states, tokens, keyboard, ARIA and examples.
- **Badge** (`aipim-badge`): a small mark with a numeral or an icon next to text, for step numbers and icon chips. Decorative (`aria-hidden`). Outline, `--filled` and `--tint`, round and `--square`, sizes 48 and `--sm` (32).
- **Card** header (`aipim-card__header`, `__heading`, `__eyebrow`): a Badge before the title and an eyebrow above it. Card tones `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--tertiary` and `--success`, painted with the `tint/*` roles.
- **Tag** styles `--inverse` (for dark or colored surfaces, it follows the text color of the surface) and `--link` (an `a`, with no remove button).
- **Tabs** behavior in `aipim.js` and `tabs.css`: a panel starts at its top. When a tab is chosen and the top of the screen is below the top edge of the list, the page scrolls so the list is at the top of the screen, always at the same height (a sticky list is read where it sits in the page); when the top of the screen is above that edge, nothing moves. In the spec, with a do and a don't, and in `tools/verify_web.py`.
- Tokens: `focus/offset` (3px, the gap of the focus ring), `action/secondary/hover`, `bg/scrim`, `link/hover`, and the tint roles `tint/primary`, `tint/secondary`, `tint/tertiary`, `tint/success` and `tint/error` with `tint/text` and `tint/text/muted` (20 new contrast checks, AAA for text and AA for supporting text).
- Generated docs for color, typography, space, shape and motion, and the tokens in CSS, JSON, Compose and Swift.

Icons and illustrations
- 63 line icons from Hugeicons Free (MIT) with `icons.json`, an SVG sprite and the build script. Five are platform logos (GitHub, Figma, npm, Claude, MCP server) for links in the documentation.
- `illustrations/`: ten illustrations for the states of the interface (welcome, empty, loading, in progress, ready, success, error, no results, offline and contact), by [Luiz Fukumoto](https://www.instagram.com/lunattox/), as SVG (880 KB in all, 260 KB gzipped, optimized with `npm run illustrations:optimize`) with `illustrations.json` (name, use, description). In the package as `aipim-ds/illustrations/svg/*`.

### Changed
- **A livelier palette.** Urucum (red-orange) is the primary, an azulejo blue the secondary, cajá yellow the accent, with a brighter leaf green and a cherry red. Buttons use the vivid mid tones: the button label meets AA (4.5:1) instead of AAA, while body text and links stay at AAA (7:1). The 84 contrast checks and the color blindness checks pass.
- The link color is the blue (`#014B95` in light, `#8CBEFE` in dark), clearly apart from the body text; the generator fails if a link is too close to the body text (color difference of at least 20).
- A smaller palette: five colors of nine tones (100 to 900) plus a neutral scale of twelve, 57 primitives instead of 79. Status icons use other tones so success, error, info and primary stay distinguishable with color blindness.
- Tabs have round top corners only, so the hover fill sits flush on the selected bar, and they scroll sideways inside the list when they do not fit (the focus ring is not clipped). The tab bar label wraps onto a second line instead of being cut.
- The Figma Card counts its 1px border in the layout, so the border shows around the media; it matches the CSS.
- Figma icons: the stroke scales with the icon like in the code (1.5px at 24, 1.25px at 20, 1px at 16), and the icon sizes follow the specs (16, 20 or 24 by component). The icon follows the color of its label (Button, Link, Tab, Tab bar, Tag), the type icon of the Alert and the Toast follows the status, and the Text field error icon is `error/icon`.
- The Illustration component in Figma has ten scenes in vector, scaling with the instance, instead of eight raster images.
- The docs follow the maintainer's copy guide: no em dash, none of the words that make a text sound generated, no lists of three, and a fact next to every claim. The English docs were revised against it.
- The component stories live in `stories/atoms`, `stories/molecules` and `stories/organisms`, with their ids pinned (`atoms-button`), and the MDX pages in `stories/en` and `stories/pt-br`.

### Fixed
